How the image becomes music

  • Brightness → melody. Brighter columns (clouds, ice, desert) climb higher. The melody leans on the notes of the current chord, like a real tune.
  • Surface → instrument. Water is played by a flute, vegetation by violins, bare land by brighter violins, cloud and ice by a celesta.
  • Busy landscapes → bigger arrangement. Calm stretches stay quiet with strings only. Colorful, varied stretches bring in harp, basses, timpani and a second violin line.
  • Coastlines and cloud edges → harp strum. You hear the border when the surface changes.
  • Move the map while it plays and the music follows the new view.

Imagery from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "The Earth Information Jukebox", by team Earthtone. All sound is synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API.
